# Evidence-Based Insights

Generated by the analytical pipeline.

1. Health Beauty is the largest revenue category at 1,255,695, representing 9.3% of merchandise revenue.
2. SP is the largest customer state by revenue at 5,163,867, with 41,125 orders.
3. Repeat customers account for 3.0% of customers in the observed dataset.
4. Average order value is 137.42 based on 98,199 sales-eligible orders with item revenue.
5. On-time delivery is 93.2% among orders with both delivered and estimated delivery dates.
6. Orders classified as late have an average review score lower than on-time orders (2.35 vs 4.28); this is an association, not proof of causality.

## Recommendations

1. Protect availability and merchandising for Health Beauty, which contributes 9.3% of revenue.
2. Prioritize operational review for SP because it is the highest-revenue customer state; compare demand with its delivery performance before changing capacity.
3. Use repeat-customer analysis to identify retention opportunities, but avoid treating descriptive segments as causal or predictive without further validation.
4. Investigate late-delivery orders as a customer-experience risk because late orders have a lower observed review score; validate with additional operational data before making causal claims.

## Analytical caution

These findings describe the observed Olist dataset. They are not current Brazilian market statistics and do not establish causal relationships.
