# Business Questions

## Executive
1. How does revenue change over time?
2. Which categories and states contribute most revenue?
3. How does average order value vary?

## Customer
4. What share of customers purchase more than once?
5. Which customer segments have the highest observed value?
6. How does repeat behaviour vary by acquisition period?

## Product
7. Which categories combine high revenue with high freight burden?
8. Which categories have weaker review scores?

## Operations
9. What percentage of delivered orders arrive by the estimated date?
10. Where are severe delays concentrated?
11. How does delivery performance differ by state?

## Customer experience
12. How are review scores distributed?
13. Are longer delivery delays associated with lower review scores?

Every final answer must include the metric definition, population/grain, observation, interpretation, and limitation.
